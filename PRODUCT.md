# SI MENU Product Context

## Register

Product

## Product

SI MENU is the SUPAVUT GROUP intranet portal and application gateway. It brings together internal applications, company announcements, official documents, calendars, maps, and employee information in one place.

## Users

- SUPAVUT GROUP employees who need fast access to internal systems and current company information
- HR staff who publish announcements and official documents
- IT staff who maintain the portal and support employees

## Purpose

Make official company information and internal work systems easy to find, current, and dependable while reducing repeated steps across separate channels.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, minimal, clear, and trustworthy. The interface should use SUPAVUT blue and green with neutral surfaces, strong whitespace, and restrained motion.

## Anti-References

- Crowded legacy portals with many competing colors
- Decorative cards or gradients that reduce information clarity
- Dense tables without readable spacing or mobile overflow handling
- Excessive motion, rounded containers, or ornamental UI

## Design Principles

1. One section communicates one primary purpose.
2. Official information and actions take priority over decoration.
3. Use consistent typography, spacing, focus states, and responsive behavior.
4. Preserve Thai, English, and Myanmar language support where the source content allows it.
5. Keep source-of-truth data current without unnecessary manual duplication.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and horizontal overflow for wide official tables on narrow screens. Preserve the official Thai source text when no approved translation exists.
